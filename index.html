How Koyantaw Works

1

Setup Your Safety Network

Add trusted contacts who will receive alerts when you need help. These can be family members, friends, or emergency contacts.

2

Start Your Journey

When you begin your commute, activate the app. Koyantaw will automatically monitor your movement and surroundings.

3

Smart Monitoring

The app continuously analyzes your speed, movement patterns. If anything unusual is detected, it will trigger an alert.

4

Panic Mode

If you feel unsafe, simply tap the panic button. This immediately sends your location and activates emergency protocols to your safety network.

5

WatchMeOver

Share your live location with trusted contacts who can monitor your journey in real-time and respond quickly if needed.

6

Safe Arrival

Once you reach your destination, the app automatically notifies your safety network that you've arrived safely.
