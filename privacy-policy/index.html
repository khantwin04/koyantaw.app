Privacy Policy

Effective date: April 14, 2026

Koyantaw ("we", "our", "us") is a personal safety app that helps users share live trip updates, send panic alerts, and keep trusted contacts informed. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and what choices you have.

Back to Home

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: email address, phone number, user ID, and account creation date.
  • Trusted contact information: names and phone numbers for contacts you add to your safety network.
  • Location and trip data: current location, destination, route progress, check-in status, panic session location, and related timestamps while safety sharing features are active.
  • Notification data: Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) device tokens and notification topic subscriptions used to deliver safety alerts.
  • Device-stored security data: panic code and security keys are stored locally using secure device storage.

2. How We Use Information

  • Provide core app safety features (WatchMeOver and Panic Mode).
  • Share your safety status with contacts you explicitly select.
  • Send emergency and session notifications.
  • Maintain session state, account access, and reliability.
  • Improve safety workflows and service quality.

3. When We Share Information

  • With your chosen contacts: when you start sharing, trigger panic mode, or send check-ins.
  • With service providers: Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Cloud Messaging) and Google Maps Platform services used by the app (such as Places and Geocoding).
  • For legal reasons: if required by law or valid legal process.

4. Data Retention

  • Active location-sharing session history is retained for a limited period after a session ends (default setting is 24 hours, depending on app configuration).
  • Account and contact records are kept while your account is active, unless you remove them.
  • Panic code and certain security data are stored on your device and managed locally.

5. Permissions and Controls

  • Location permission: used for live safety tracking, including background updates during active sessions.
  • Contacts permission: used only to let you choose trusted contacts.
  • Notification permission: used for safety alerts and important session updates.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect personal data. Data is transmitted over encrypted network channels. No security method is 100% guaranteed, but we continuously work to improve protection.

7. Analytics and Advertising

As of this policy date, Koyantaw does not use advertising SDKs for behavioral advertising and does not sell your personal data.

8. Children's Privacy

Koyantaw is not intended for children under 13. If we learn that we have collected personal data from a child under 13 without appropriate consent, we will take steps to delete that information.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version on this page with a revised effective date.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the Koyantaw team through the official support channel listed for the app.
