Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 10, 2026

Koyantaw ("we", "our", "us") is a personal safety app that helps users share live trip updates, send panic alerts, and keep trusted contacts informed. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and what choices you have.

You choose who sees Your safety status goes only to contacts you select.
Short history Trip history is deleted 24 hours after a trip ends. Guard Mode keeps only the last hour.
Kept on your device Your panic code and security keys stay in secure device storage.
Never sold No behavioral advertising, and we don't sell your personal data.

1Information We Collect

  • Account information: your name, email address and user ID from the account you sign in with (such as Google or Apple), the phone number you add so your contacts can find you, and your account creation date.
  • Trusted contact information: names and phone numbers for contacts you add to your safety network.
  • Location and trip data: current location, speed and heading, destination, route progress, check-in status, panic session location, and related timestamps. We collect this only while Guard Mode, a WatchMeOver trip or Panic Mode is active.
  • Saved places: destinations you choose to save in the app.
  • Notification data: push notification tokens for your device and the alerts we have sent you.
  • Feedback you send us: feature requests and votes, and the optional reason you give when deleting your account.
  • Device-stored security data: panic code and security keys are stored locally using secure device storage and are not sent to us.

2How We Use Information

  • Provide core app safety features: Guard Mode, WatchMeOver and Panic Mode.
  • Detect possible danger during an active session, such as a sudden stop that may be a crash, a lost connection, or a detour from your route, and alert your contacts.
  • Share your safety status with contacts you explicitly select.
  • Send emergency and session notifications.
  • Maintain session state, account access, and reliability.
  • Improve safety workflows and service quality.

3When We Share Information

  • With your chosen contacts: when you start a WatchMeOver trip, turn Guard Mode on or off, trigger Panic Mode, send a check-in, or when an automatic safety alert fires. In Guard Mode, contacts are told that you are protected or may need help, but your location is not shown to them.
  • With service providers: Microsoft Azure (hosting for our servers), Firebase Authentication (sign-in), Firebase Cloud Messaging (push notifications), and Google Maps Platform (maps, place search and geocoding). They process data only to provide these services to us.
  • For legal reasons: if required by law or valid legal process.

4Data Retention

  • WatchMeOver trips: location history is deleted 24 hours after the trip ends.
  • Guard Mode: we keep only the most recent hour of location while protection is on, and older points are deleted continuously.
  • Notifications: your in-app alert history is kept for 90 days.
  • Account, contacts and saved places: kept while your account is active, unless you remove them.
  • Panic code and certain security data are stored on your device and managed locally.

5Permissions and Controls

  • Location permission: used for live safety tracking, including background updates while Guard Mode or a trip is active.
  • Contacts permission: used only to let you choose trusted contacts.
  • Notification permission: used for safety alerts and important session updates.

6Your Choices

  • Stop sharing at any time: turn off Guard Mode or end a trip, and location collection stops.
  • Manage your contacts: add or remove trusted contacts and saved places in the app.
  • Delete your account: you can delete your account in the app's settings. This permanently removes your account and the data linked to it from our servers.
  • Ask us: to request a copy of your data or ask a privacy question, email contact@koyantaw.app.

7Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect personal data. Data is transmitted over encrypted network channels. No security method is 100% guaranteed, but we continuously work to improve protection.

8Analytics and Advertising

As of this policy date, Koyantaw does not use advertising SDKs for behavioral advertising and does not sell your personal data.

9Children's Privacy

Koyantaw is not intended for children under 13. If we learn that we have collected personal data from a child under 13 without appropriate consent, we will take steps to delete that information.

10Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version on this page with a revised effective date.

11Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, email us at contact@koyantaw.app.
